# Toy policy: lumbar MRI

This policy is invented for the demo. It is not a payer medical policy.

Required before a packet can be drafted as complete:

- Member name and member ID
- Ordering provider name and NPI
- Requested service name and CPT
- Planned date
- Documented conservative care of at least 6 weeks, or a documented red flag
- Prior imaging attached, or an explicit statement that none exists

If any required item is missing, the packet status is `incomplete` and the missing item is listed. Do not invent the missing item.
